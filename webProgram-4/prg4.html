ILAHIA COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING AND TECHNOLOGY

Ilahia College of Engineering and Technology (ICET) is a prominent self-financing technical institution established in 2002 in Mulavoor, Muvattupuzha, Kerala. Managed by the Ilahia Trust, the college is approved by the AICTE and affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Kerala Technological University (KTU)
